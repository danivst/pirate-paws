The game

Pirate Paws is a cozy, adventure-filled game for Android, where you step into the paws of a mischievous fisher-pirate cat. Set sail across vibrant seas and explore a variety of beautiful, unique islands. As you navigate the open ocean, one of the crucial tasks you have to complete is collecting the garbage that appears in the ocean, helping achieve a cleaner environment. Alongside that, you'll catch fish, and sell your bounty to earn coins. Use your earnings to unlock fun upgrades, customize your boat, and gain unique perks to make your adventures even more exciting. With relaxing gameplay, stunning environments, and a heartwarming story, Pirate Paws offers an experience that combines the joys of exploration with not only the thrill of discovery, but also the ability to protect and clean the ocean, making every adventure feel meaningful.

The team

At the heart of Pirate Paws is a small but passionate team of two students, dedicated to creating a cozy experience for players. We share love for crafting engaging games that bring joy to people's lives and aim to create a game that combines relaxing exploration with meaningful environmental impact. We believe in the power of games to not only entertain but to inspire change and bring awareness to important causes, like ocean conservation. Both of us poured our creativity, skills, and love for gaming into every aspect of Pirate Paws—from the assets creation to the relaxing gameplay mechanics. We hope you enjoy playing as much as we enjoyed making it!
You can reach out to us in case of inquiries at: pirate-paws@gmail.com

The cause

Our mission is to spread awareness about the importance of environmental care and to show that even the smallest actions, like picking up a piece of garbage from the sidewalk or cleaning up the ocean, can make a big difference. We believe that every action counts, and through our game, we hope to inspire players to take part in making the world a cleaner, greener place. We are proud to support The Ocean Cleanup and their mission to reduce ocean pollution, and we aim to engage children and families by creating a fun, interactive experience that highlights the importance of protecting our oceans. By raising awareness through gameplay, we hope to encourage future generations to take action and become advocates for a healthier planet. Together, we can make waves of change—one small step at a time.
