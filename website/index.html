Welcome to Pirate Paws!

Description

Pirate Paws is a cozy mobile game made for Android. In it you play as a fisher-pirate cat - explore the ocean (and clean it too!) and all the different islands you can discover. Your goal is to catch fish, then sell them and collect money in order to buy different types of upgrades and perks. Discover the world of Pirate Paws to find out more!
